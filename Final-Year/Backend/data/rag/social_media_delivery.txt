Social Media Content Delivery

A social media content plan can connect publishing themes, formats, channels, owners, and review dates to a business's stated goals and audience. A content calendar helps coordinate planned posts; it does not guarantee reach, engagement, or sales.

Possible deliverables include a channel and content audit, messaging themes, a draft calendar, reusable post formats, and a review process. Recommend these only when they fit information explicitly supplied about the business or a clearly supported research opportunity.
